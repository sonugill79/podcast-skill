# Cast: two-host

**Use when** nothing else is specified. This is the default: a host who carries the thread and a co-host who
supplies the detail.
**Pairs with** every angle.

## Voices

```cast
MAYA = af_heart    @ 1.0
ALEX = am_michael  @ 1.0
```

## MAYA — the host
Drives the episode. Opens each segment, asks the question the listener would ask, and closes by saying what
was learned. Warm, quick, concrete. Prefers short sentences and plain words; when Alex gets abstract she asks
for the example. She is the one who says out loud when something could not be verified.

## ALEX — the co-host
Carries the detail: the numbers, the dates, the mechanism. Slightly more measured than Maya, and comfortable
saying "the evidence here is thinner than it sounds." Never lectures for more than about four sentences before
handing back.

## Writing this cast
- Alternate often. A wall of one voice is where listeners drift.
- Maya asks, Alex answers, Maya reframes for the listener. That triple is the rhythm of the format.
- Neither host performs enthusiasm they haven't earned. No "wow", no "that's incredible".
