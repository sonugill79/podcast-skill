# sources.md: should Ashgrove swap its night buses for on-demand shuttles? (FICTIONAL eval fixture)

Everything here is invented for the eval suite: the city, its transit office, the riders' union, the people and every
figure. The URLs use the reserved `example.org` domain. Rows are numbered so a judge can cite them (`V3`, `R1`, `D1`).

Question shape: **proposition**. "Should Ashgrove replace its night bus routes with on-demand shuttles, city-wide?"

## Verified

| # | Claim | Source |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | In 2025 Ashgrove ran a twelve-month pilot that replaced three night bus routes in one district with on-demand shuttles | Ashgrove Transit Office, *Night shuttle pilot: final report*, 2026 — https://example.org/ashgrove/night-pilot [primary] |
| V2 | On-demand shuttle: a van booked by app or phone call that picks riders up at a corner within four hundred metres of them, not at their door | same report, §1 [primary] |
| V3 | Average wait for a shuttle was eleven minutes. The night buses it replaced ran every thirty minutes (the headway: the time between buses). The report gives the average wait only, not its spread | same report, §3 [primary] |
| V4 | Cost to the city per trip: fourteen dollars by shuttle, against nine dollars by night bus. About a fifth of the shuttle figure was one-off software set-up | same report, §5 and annex B [primary] |
| V5 | Night ridership in the pilot district rose twenty-two percent | same report, §3 [primary] |
| V6 | Eighty-eight percent of trips were booked in the app; twelve percent by phone call | same report, §4 [primary] |
| V7 | Riders aged over sixty-five fell from fifteen percent of night riders to nine percent (rider survey, four hundred and twelve responses) | same report, §6 [primary] |
| V8 | The report's own caveat, quote: "One district, one year, and a mild winter." | same report, §7 (checked verbatim) [primary] |
| V9 | Transit Office position, quote: "Nobody should wait half an hour at midnight." Pilot lead Sam Porter, still in post and still holding the position (2026 council minutes) | same report, foreword; council minutes, March 2026 — https://example.org/ashgrove/council-2026-03 [primary] |
| V10 | Ashgrove Riders Union position, quote: "A bus you can see is a bus you can trust." Chair Lena Park, statement of February 2026 | Ashgrove Riders Union statement — https://example.org/ashgrove-riders/statement-2026-02 [primary] |
