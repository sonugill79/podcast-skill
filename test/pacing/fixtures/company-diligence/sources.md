# sources.md: Lantern Robotics as a three-year partner (FICTIONAL eval fixture)

Everything here is invented for the eval suite: the company, its customer, its people and every figure. The URLs use
the reserved `example.org` domain. Rows are numbered so a judge can cite them (`V3`, `R1`, `D1`).

Lens: a warehouse operator deciding whether to depend on Lantern Robotics for three years. Not investment advice.

| # | Claim | Source |

| V2 | 2025 revenue: forty-eight million dollars | same report, income statement [primary] |

| V5 | Cash at the end of 2025: twenty-two million dollars. At 2025's operating loss that lasts about two years (derived: 22 ÷ 11; the report gives no forecast) | same report, balance sheet [primary] + arithmetic |
